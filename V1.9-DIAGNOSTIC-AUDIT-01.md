# V1.9 Diagnostic Audit 01 — Better Diagnosis Before More Content

Date: 2026-08-30

## Decision

V1.9 starts with **diagnostic evidence orchestration**, not a new large test bank.

The site already produces useful evidence through:

- Quick Placement: Vocabulary 6 / Grammar 6 / Reading 6 / Listening 6;
- timed Reading Mini Tests MR01–MR04;
- timed Listening Mini Tests ML01–ML04;
- Full Mock MA01 + MA02 Reading/Listening section results;
- Writing productive first/retry evidence;
- Speaking productive first/retry evidence;
- Full Mock Writing word-count evidence.

The current gap is that these signals are distributed across Placement, Progress, Mini Tests, Full Mock history and productive evidence. The learner cannot yet see whether all four skills have enough baseline evidence or what diagnostic action is missing next.

## Why not create a new Full Diagnostic immediately

V1.1 originally planned longer skill-specific diagnostic sessions:

- Reading: 20–25 min;
- Listening: 15–20 min;
- Writing: 20–40 min;
- Speaking: 10–15 min;
- sessions may be split across multiple sittings.

Existing Mini Tests are shorter, and productive Writing/Speaking evidence is not equivalent to a human-scored IELTS exam. Therefore V1.9 Batch 1 must **not** relabel existing short forms as a complete exam-equivalent diagnostic or output one overall IELTS band.

Instead, Batch 1 establishes a transparent evidence-coverage layer. Dedicated diagnostic assets should be added only after the remaining evidence gap is measured.

## Batch 1 evidence rules

### Starting profile

Quick Placement remains the short starting signal. It is required before the four-skill baseline is called ready.

### Reading

- baseline evidence: at least 1 distinct timed Reading Mini Test or Full Mock Reading form;
- broader evidence: at least 2 distinct timed forms;
- raw results remain learning evidence, not an overall IELTS estimate.

### Listening

- baseline evidence: at least 1 distinct timed Listening Mini Test or Full Mock Listening form;
- broader evidence: at least 2 distinct timed forms;
- audio status remains governed by the existing production/fallback rules.

### Writing

- baseline evidence: at least 1 `first` productive attempt with **150+ words**;
- broader evidence: at least 2 substantial first attempts, or one complete Full Mock Writing section where Task 1 is 150+ words and Task 2 is 250+ words;
- no automatic Writing band is created.

### Speaking

- baseline evidence: at least 1 `first` productive attempt with **60+ transcript words**;
- broader evidence: substantial first attempts from at least 2 distinct prompt/block sources;
- Full Mock Speaking beta completion alone is not counted because it stores completion but no transcript/quality evidence.

## Learner flow

`Quick Placement → Reading timed baseline → Listening timed baseline → Writing substantial first attempt → Speaking substantial first attempt → return to Today`

Already-existing evidence satisfies the relevant step automatically. Learners should never be forced to redo valid evidence only to satisfy the Diagnostic Center.

Primary fallback actions for a missing baseline:

- Placement → Quick Placement;
- Reading → MR01;
- Listening → ML01;
- Writing → WT1-05 full Task 1 workspace;
- Speaking → SPB01 Speaking Practice Bank.

## Architecture guardrails

The first Diagnostic Center is read-only:

- no new localStorage key;
- no learner-data schema migration;
- no new scoring system;
- no new MutationObserver;
- no polling interval;
- shared V1.5 render lifecycle only;
- existing Mini Test / Mock / productive evidence remains source of truth.

## Acceptance

1. Progress shows Quick Placement readiness plus four skill evidence cards.
2. `Missing / Baseline evidence / Broader evidence` states are derived from existing storage.
3. One dominant next missing diagnostic action is shown.
4. 149-word Writing and 59-word Speaking attempts do not satisfy their gates.
5. 150+ Writing and 60+ Speaking first attempts do satisfy their gates.
6. Speaking Mock beta completion alone does not satisfy Speaking.
7. Two distinct Reading/Listening timed forms upgrade evidence breadth.
8. Complete Mock Writing upgrades Writing evidence breadth.
9. Once Placement + all four baselines exist, the primary action returns to Today instead of demanding more testing.
10. No fake overall IELTS band is displayed.

## After Batch 1

Use the resulting coverage model to decide whether V1.9 needs dedicated longer diagnostic assets. The likely unresolved question is not Reading/Listening quantity but whether Writing Task 2 and Speaking need more standardized first-attempt collection before the profile can be considered sufficiently broad.
