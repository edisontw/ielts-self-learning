## 5. Reading structure — 4 / 4 reviewed

| Question | Actual decision | Retry boundary | Route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `R02-Q3` | Identify a conclusion drawn from a limitation | direct Core Retry | none |
| `R02-Q7` | Map paragraph as obvious solution → limitation → refined solution | direct Core Retry | none |
| `MR01-Q10` | Locate the paragraph that presents an objection and then responds to it | Mini Test; no single-question Retry | **R02 → QR05** |
| `MA01-R08` | Explain why trees and green roofs are mentioned: alternative / complementary heat strategies | Full Mock; no single-question Retry | **R02 → QR03** |

### Why one generic structure route is unsafe

The two transfer errors share a structural-reading skill but use different retrieval decisions:

- `MR01-Q10` is a **Matching Information / paragraph-function locator** task. QR05 is the exact Lab because it asks the learner to locate a requested information function.
- `MA01-R08` is a **Reading Multiple Choice** role-of-example task. QR03 is the exact Lab because it trains evidence-first option comparison and distinguishes a structural role from distractor options.

Both use R02 as the Core review because R02 explicitly teaches claim, evidence, example, contrast, limitation, and conclusion roles.

### Compatibility strategy

`reading-structure-routing-v16.js` stores only the two audited non-retriable IDs:

- `MR01-Q10 → matching-information`
- `MA01-R08 → multiple-choice`

The classifier also accepts explicit future `questionType` metadata when it is one of those audited semantics.

Unknown structure IDs remain unrouted rather than being guessed into a Lab. Existing R02 lesson errors keep direct Retry and receive no competing reuse CTA.
