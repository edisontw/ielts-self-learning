## Learner-facing behavior now closed

Today no longer presents Adaptive Today, Study Plan, returned AI feedback and Productive Evidence as several equally prominent cards.

The orchestrator ranks already-existing actions in this order:

1. due spaced review;
2. current Study Plan session;
3. pending AI feedback needing revision/retry;
4. productive retry signal;
5. prerequisite-safe adaptive new material.

Only the highest-ranked candidate remains a full-size card and is labelled `Do this now`. Other distinct destinations are reduced to at most two compact alternatives, with duplicate destinations collapsed.

Fresh users without Placement are deliberately excluded from orchestration so Quick Placement onboarding remains unchanged.
