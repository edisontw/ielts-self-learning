## Goal

Resolve the strongest remaining below-threshold Listening watch signal without creating a route or Repair lesson merely because it appears in two Mini Test forms.

Reviewed questions:

- `ML02-Q9` — severe weather → participants receive an email and the photography session moves indoors;
- `ML04-Q10` — severe weather makes the river unsafe → the activity moves to the environmental laboratory rather than being cancelled.

Both questions ask the learner to track a stated condition to its resulting action. The historical raw tag `listening-final-meaning` is therefore broader than the actual cognitive process.

## Semantic decision

Normalize both signals to:

`listening-conditional-outcome`

Compatibility mapping:

- `ML02-Q9` → `listening-conditional-outcome`
- `ML04-Q10` → `listening-conditional-outcome`
- legacy ML02 / ML04 `listening-final-meaning` history normalizes by test form;
- unknown legacy IDs/forms are not guessed.

No destructive raw question-bank migration is required. The shared Mini Test semantic normalizer remains the source of truth for result display, Error Notebook persistence, trend analysis, and audits.

## Existing teaching owner

A deeper owner review corrects the earlier conservative judgement that this micro-skill lacked exact teaching coverage.

`L04 — Don't Fall for the Distractor` explicitly teaches:

- `possible option → correction / contrast → FINAL meaning`;
- a `conditional option`: `If X happens, we'll…, otherwise…`;
- the rule that IELTS often tests the completed meaning rather than the first plausible phrase.

This is a direct semantic match for ML02-Q9 and ML04-Q10. L04 is therefore the **teaching owner** for conditional-outcome interpretation.

`QL01 — Listening Multiple Choice` reinforces final option-state tracking, but is not treated as a new route destination in this pass because the transfer evidence remains below the action threshold.

## Routing decision

**No new existing-practice route. No LR Repair.**

Reason:

- only two independent Test forms currently carry this micro-skill;
- both questions are semantically coherent, so there is no false recurrence to suppress;
- the new label improves learner-facing diagnostic precision without converting a low-frequency watch signal into an intervention;
- route/Repair creation remains evidence-gated rather than frequency-driven.

Current status:

`WATCH-OWNER — listening-conditional-outcome ×2 forms — owner L04 — no runtime CTA`
