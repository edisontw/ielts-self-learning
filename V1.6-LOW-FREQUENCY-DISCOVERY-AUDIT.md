## Goal

Move below the recurring-family threshold only as a discovery exercise. Review one-off and two-off Mini Test / Full Mock signals without creating routes or Repair lessons from frequency alone.

Decision rule:

`low-frequency Test/Mock signal → inspect actual question semantics → prevent false recurrence → watch for future same-skill evidence`

A runtime change is justified only when the current semantic label can already create incorrect learner-facing diagnostics.

## Main finding: one false two-form recurrence

Two Reading Mini Test questions share the historical raw tag `reading-definition`:

- `MR02-Q4` asks what the passage-defined term **useful shade** means. This is explicit definition retrieval.
- `MR04-Q4` asks what **distinction** the writer makes between high borrowing activity and actual material-use reduction. This is concept comparison / distinction, not definition retrieval.

Mini Test trends mark a pattern as recurring when the same tag appears across at least **two different forms**. A learner who misses both questions can therefore be shown a false recurring `reading-definition` weakness even though the two errors involve different cognitive processes.

### Decision

Normalize the historical umbrella before persistence, trend detection, and audits:

- `MR02-Q4` → `reading-explicit-definition`
- `MR04-Q4` → `reading-distinction`

Historical saved `reading-definition` evidence is normalized by `testId` for MR02 and MR04. Unknown legacy IDs/forms are not guessed.

No raw question-bank migration is required in this pass. The compatibility normalizer remains the source of truth for learner-facing Mini Test error semantics.

## Why `listening-final-meaning` is not routed yet

The two questions are semantically close:

- `ML02-Q9`: severe weather → email + indoor gallery session;
- `ML04-Q10`: unsafe river conditions → move to environmental laboratory.

Both require tracking a conditional outcome to the final stated action. However:

- there are only two independent forms;
- no existing Core/Lab lesson owns this exact conditional-outcome micro-skill cleanly;
- forcing the pair into `listening-final-decision`, `listening-detail`, or `listening-scope` would broaden those families beyond their reviewed semantics.

The correct action is to keep the tag visible as a **watch candidate**. A third independent transfer signal should trigger a semantics-first owner/route/Repair review.

## Why the spelling signal does not get a route yet

`MA01-L01` asks the learner to capture a spelled surname. QL02 explicitly teaches form/notes completion accuracy and includes `listening-spelling` as an instructional error target.

This is an exact teaching match, but the Test/Mock evidence currently contains only one Full Mock signal. The current below-threshold audit contract is discovery-only, so no runtime route is added from a single occurrence.

## Why `academic-vocabulary` is not a recurring family

The two Full Mock items are:

- `MA01-L33` — capture the named term `transpiration` from Listening;
- `MA01-R24` — complete `right to ______` with `repair` from Reading.

They differ by skill and task process. The second answer is also ordinary task vocabulary rather than evidence of advanced academic lexical knowledge. Their shared raw tag should not be interpreted as a same-skill recurring weakness.

No runtime change is needed because Full Mock recurrence is not currently inferred from this two-item cross-skill count.
